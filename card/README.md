# Amir Ghorbani — digital business card

Published path: https://amirghorbani.dev/card/

Standalone page with local Three.js 0.180.0 (MIT license in vendor/LICENSE), original business card artwork, and the existing portfolio logo from ../assets/ag-logo-112.png. No portfolio styles or scripts are imported.

Drag to rotate, flip, reset, or switch to flat view. Reduced-motion preference starts in flat view; WebGL failure also falls back to artwork. Rendering pauses when idle, hidden or offscreen. The camera fits the rotation envelope to prevent edge clipping.

Artwork: 2400px lossless WebP exported from original Illustrator artboards. Contact download: Amir Ghorbani, mobile +6421631023, email and portfolio URL. All contact actions are available without WebGL.
